Church management software.Questions and answers.

Which languages does Avenit support?

English, Polish and Ukrainian. Every user picks their own language in the app, in the browser and on the phone.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes. Avenit works in the browser and as a mobile app for iOS and Android, with push notifications for members and volunteer teams.

Is Avenit a good fit for a small church?

Yes. Modules are enabled one by one, so a small congregation can start with the member directory, calendar and volunteer schedule, then add more as the community grows. The interface is simple and requires no training.

How much does the church app cost?

From 79 zł a month, VAT included. The price depends on the number of adults in your member directory, and every module is in every plan. See the pricing. To start, book a demo through the contact form and get a trial period with no credit card required.

Is Avenit GDPR-compliant?

Yes. Every church gets its own separate database, data is stored in the EU, and we sign a data processing agreement with each community. Children’s data is protected by a check-in system with pickup security codes. Details in our privacy policy (PL).

How is Avenit different from Planning Center?

Avenit keeps your data in the European Union (GDPR) and covers in one system what Planning Center splits across paid add-ons or external tools: finances, a team messenger, email/SMS/push campaigns, a prayer wall and small groups, alongside service planning and volunteer scheduling.
